### What changed
- Added **XXVII · Holonomy**.
- The chamber renders a unit sphere with a closed great-circle triangle.
- One vertex is the north pole, one is fixed on the equator, and the third moves along the equator with pointer x-position.
- A warm traveler carries a tangent vector around the loop.
- Each edge uses the exact great-circle axis `normalize(p × q)` and angle `acos(p · q)`.
- The traveler position and tangent vector are transported by the same 3D rotation, so the arrow is not steered by an authored correction.
- The returned vector is compared with the original tangent direction at the north pole.
- For this right-right-`α` spherical triangle, the measured holonomy equals the enclosed unit-sphere area `α`.
- Click locks/unlocks triangle geometry; **Space** performs or cancels a full circuit; **R** restores the canonical loop.
- Added the room page, Atlas/front-door entries, continuity update, architecture note, and room registration.
